**Quantitative Differences between Real and Fake Image**

The **real image** shows stronger and more diverse gradients, higher contrast variability, higher entropy, and asymmetric pixel distributions. These are signatures of natural image formation caused by optics, lighting, and sensor noise.

The **fake image** is smoother, brighter, more uniform, and statistically balanced — typical of AI-generated content where models suppress extreme edges and normalize textures to improve visual appeal.
